The premise

Nonlinear is a daytime series about ordinary life after the singularity. It starts in an office park in Silicon Valley, and then it keeps going: into kitchens, cul-de-sacs, dentists' waiting rooms, the bottom of the sea, and a few places that weren't there yesterday.

Each scene works like a single cartoon panel. There's one impossible picture, and everyone in it reacts like it's a Tuesday. Cows get product updates. Houseplants file grievances. Time moves sideways. Nobody raises their voice.
